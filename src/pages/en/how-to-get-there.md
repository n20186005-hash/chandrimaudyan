Chandrima Udyan sits in Sher-e-Bangla Nagar, Dhaka, on the shore of Crescent Lake beside the National Parliament building. Maps list the address as “Crecent, Lake Road, Dhaka”. Below are several practical ways to reach it — each with both its advantages and its limitations, so you can pick the one that suits your starting point.

## Metro (MRT Line 6)

If you are already inside Dhaka, MRT Line 6 is usually the most reliable way to avoid the traffic. According to the DMTCL station list, both **[Bijoy Sarani](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/)** and **[Agargaon](/en/metro-agargaon/)** stations fall within the near part of the park.

- **Bijoy Sarani station:** generally treated as the closest station. From there you can either walk or take a short rickshaw or CNG ride.
- **Agargaon station:** a little further away. If you would rather not walk, a rickshaw, CNG or ride-share works well.

> Exact walking distances, which exit is most convenient and which road is safest all vary from day to day. Check the current station connections and timings with DMTCL before you set out.

### Starting from Bijoy Sarani station

Leave the station and head towards the parliament building and Crescent Lake, and you will reach the near side of the park. Walking routes, the condition of the footpaths and which direction is safe to cross all change from day to day, so follow local guidance and traffic signals. If time is short, taking a rickshaw or CNG after you exit makes the last stretch quicker.

### Starting from Agargaon station

The distance is longer from here, so allow time if you plan to walk. Otherwise a rickshaw, CNG or ride-share can carry you past the parliament area to the park. During the late afternoon peak this stretch moves slowly.

## Bus, rickshaw and CNG

- **Bus:** take a bus heading towards Farmgate, Agargaon or the parliament area, get off, and cover the final stretch by rickshaw or CNG. Bus routes and stops nearby change often.
- **Rickshaw:** cheap and convenient for short distances, but slow at busy times.
- **CNG:** agree the fare first and state the destination clearly as “Chandrima Udyan” or “Crescent Lake”.
- **Ride-share:** the least to think about, since the app handles navigation; however, security rules around the parliament area may require the car to stop at a specific point.

### Practical notes on fares and timing

No fixed fare is quoted here, because it changes with time of day, route and negotiation. Agree on the destination and fare before you get in. At busy times even a short distance can take much longer, so allow extra time for the return trip too. On rainy days wet roads and slower traffic stretch the journey further.

## Driving and parking

Do not assume there is a large dedicated car park for visitors inside the park. Leave your vehicle where traffic and security instructions allow. See the [separate guide](/en/parking-and-dropoff/) for details.

## Step by step to the park

1. Decide your starting point, and use the metro if that is feasible.
2. Get off at Bijoy Sarani (or Agargaon) and exit the station following its signage.
3. Head towards the parliament building and Crescent Lake, watching for signposts and notices.
4. If you need to cross a road, use traffic signals and a footbridge or marked crossing.
5. At the park entrance, read the notice at the gate and go in.

## Choosing an option — at a glance

| Situation | Suggestion |
| --- | --- |
| Inside the city, short on time | Metro to Bijoy Sarani, then a short rickshaw or CNG ride |
| Travelling with children or older companions | Ride-share or your own car — you can cut the walking at both ends |
| Tight budget | Bus plus rickshaw; agree the fare first |
| Heavy luggage | Ride-share or a licensed taxi |
| Arriving from far away | Combine a long-distance terminal with the metro or a ride-share |

## Limited mobility and older companions

If walking is limited, or you are travelling with older companions, it helps to be dropped off as close to the gate as possible, which cuts the walking at both ends. A ride-share or your own car is best for that, since you can ask to stop where you prefer. The current condition of internal paths, ramps and rest areas cannot be stated with certainty in advance, so ask at the entrance; the [separate accessibility guide](/en/accessibility/) covers this in more detail.

## Once you arrive

- After getting out of the vehicle, check the map for the permitted entrance; there may be checkpoints near the parliament building and the Military Museum.
- It is convenient to arrive in daylight and plan to leave before dark.
- If you return the same way, allow extra time for traffic; be especially careful on journeys after dusk.

### Entrances and security checks

Because the park stands close to the National Parliament building and the Military Museum, there may be security checks or temporary barricades on some days. Do not disregard these instructions; use the route the security staff point out. Keep valuables with you and do not leave bags open in a crowd.

## In short

Metro to Bijoy Sarani plus a short walk or rickshaw ride is the simplest plan from inside the city. Allow extra time for security checks, peak hours and wet roads, and read the notice at the entrance once you arrive.
