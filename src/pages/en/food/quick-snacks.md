While walking in the park many people feel like eating something light — especially when with family or children, a quick and low-cost snack is useful. In the Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Bijoy Sarani area there are bakeries and snack shops, which are convenient for takeaway and a quick meal. Below is what kind of options there are, how to arrange a budget and where to go afterwards.

> **Important:** the names below are those of locally known establishments — they are not a recommendation. Their **current opening and closing status, menus and prices have not been separately verified**; check for yourself before you go.

## Types of quick snacks and familiar names

| Establishment | Generally known as | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tasty Treat – Bijoy Sarani | Fast food and sweets | Times, prices, ordering method |
| Cooper's Tejgaon | Bakery-type food | Opening/closing, menu |
| Local bakeries in the area | Light snacks and takeaway | Times, cleanliness |

- **Tasty Treat – Bijoy Sarani:** a familiar name for fast food and sweets; worth considering for a quick order.
- **Cooper's Tejgaon:** known as a bakery and food place.
- **Local bakeries and shops:** light food such as pitha, bread and biscuits for eating on the go.

## Budget and takeaway

- **To keep costs low:** settle on a particular budget and share out a few small items.
- **Takeaway:** it saves time and there is no need to sit inside repeatedly — you can eat sitting in the park.
- **Cleanliness:** do not throw food wrappers or peel in the park; use the designated bins.
- **Water:** on a hot day it is important to arrange water; especially for children.

## The route from the park

```
Chandrima Udyan (Crescent Lake Road) → the road alongside → a bakery/snack shop
```

- **On foot:** leaving the park and following the road alongside, you can look for food shops; walking in daylight is safest.
- **Rickshaw/CNG:** can be used to find a shop quickly or to go to the market; agree the fare first.
- **Ride-share:** if you do not know the brand name, give the area name as the destination and get off nearby.

> No specific walking minutes or distance are assumed here; they change with traffic and the state of the route.

### The way from the park to the shops

Leaving the park and following the road alongside, you can look for food shops; walking in daylight is safest. If you do not know the brand name, give the area name as the destination, get off nearby and walk. Deciding on the budget and what to buy in advance saves time.

## Which time of day it suits

- **Morning:** light snacks and bakery-type food may be convenient in the morning.
- **Afternoon:** good for eating something light in the middle of a walk.
- **Lunch/dinner:** if you want a full meal it is better to head for a [restaurant](/en/food/bijoy-sarani-restaurants/), because these shops are mainly for light snacks.

## Where to go afterwards

- **Back to the park:** after the snack you can walk again in the park or towards Crescent Lake.
- **To the museum:** you can head towards the [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) or the [Air Force Museum](/en/nearby/air-force-museum/).
- **To the metro:** taking [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) to another part of the city; check the timings with DMTCL.

### What to verify before you go

- Check in advance the current opening and closing status, menu and prices of a particular shop.
- Be sure whether items suitable for eating on the go are available.
- To keep costs low, fix a budget and take small items.

### What to do if the shop is closed

If a shop is closed or the food has run out you can go to another nearby bakery or restaurant, or return to the park and arrange your plan. Travel takes longer in monsoon rain, so keep an alternative ready.

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### What time of day a snack is good

Light bakery-type food in the morning, and a light snack in the afternoon in the middle of a walk, are convenient. If you want a full meal at lunch or dinner it is better to head for a restaurant. To avoid waiting at busy times, go a little earlier or later.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After the snack you can take Bijoy Sarani station on MRT Line 6 to head to another part of the city. You can also return to the park and walk towards Crescent Lake; check the timings with DMTCL.

## Cleanliness and etiquette for eating in the park

- Do not throw food wrappers or peel in the park; use the designated bins.
- On a hot day carry water; especially for children.
- At busy times give others room and keep the setting peaceful.

## Who it suits and when it is useful

Light, quick food is not equally useful in every situation — choosing with your companions and the time in mind works best.

### With children

Children can easily eat light snacks taken by hand. But on a hot day keep water and small portions, and keep things clean after eating in the park.

### If you want to head out quickly

If you want to eat in a short time and go out, takeaway is convenient — there is no need to sit inside. Deciding what to buy in advance saves queueing time.

## General principles for cutting cost and staying clean

### Ways to cut cost

Fixing a budget in advance and sharing a few small items keeps costs under control. Buying in pairs or as a group reduces waste.

### While eating in the park

Do not throw food wrappers or peel in the park; use the designated bins. At busy times give others room and keep the setting peaceful.

## In short

Quick snacks and bakeries are a low-cost option for a light hunger while visiting the park — takeaway also saves time. But no shop's time, menu or prices are confirmed in this guide; verify for yourself before you go, keep things clean and arrange your plan after eating.
