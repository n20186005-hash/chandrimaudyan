After walking around Chandrima Udyan, many people want to sit down for a cup of coffee or tea. In the Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Bijoy Sarani area there are some café-style places where you can rest and chat. Below is what kind of options there may be, what time is good to go, and how to arrange your plan afterwards.

> **Important:** the names below are those of locally known establishments — they are not a recommendation. Their **current opening and closing status, menus and prices have not been separately verified**; check for yourself before you go.

- **Rüya Coffee Shop:** known as a coffee-focused name; according to the brand page it also has a branch on the Military Museum grounds.
- **SOHO CAFE:** worth considering for light food and drinks in a café-style setting.
- **Tasty Treat – Bijoy Sarani:** a familiar name for fast food and sweets, convenient for a light snack.

## An afternoon rest and chat

Enjoying the greenery of the park and the setting of Crescent Lake and then sitting in a café is a natural sequence. In the afternoon or evening when the light is soft, the time feels comfortable for a chat or a rest.

- **When:** towards the afternoon after walking — though at busy times you may have to wait for a seat.
- **What with:** a cup of coffee/tea and a light snack — enough for many people.
- **How long:** the particular rules differ from establishment to establishment; at busy times keeping it short is courteous.

## The route from the park

```
Chandrima Udyan (Crescent Lake Road) → the road alongside → a café (Bijoy Sarani/Sher-e-Bangla Nagar)
```

- **On foot:** leaving the park and following the road alongside, you find a café nearby; walking in daylight is safe.
- **Rickshaw/CNG:** convenient for arriving quickly; agree the fare first.
- **Metro:** getting off at Bijoy Sarani station, walk or take a short rickshaw.

> No specific walking minutes or distance are assumed here; they change with traffic and the state of the route.

### The way from the park to the café

Leaving the park and following the road alongside, you find a café nearby; walking in daylight is safest. If the distance is short, a rickshaw/CNG also works. Deciding in advance which café you are going to reduces both time and hesitation.

## Reservation and verification

- On an ordinary day a reservation is not always needed, but on a holiday or for a large group it is better to check in advance.
- The current times, menu or prices of any place have not been separately verified; look at them directly or online.
- Suitability for breakfast or dinner differs from establishment to establishment — verify specifically.

## Where to go afterwards

- **To the museum:** the [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) is nearby; you can go there after the coffee.
- **To the metro:** taking Bijoy Sarani station to another part of the city; check the time.
- **Back to the park:** if you want to walk in the park again in the evening light, return the same way and follow the safety instructions.

### What to verify before you go

- Check in advance the current opening and closing status, menu and prices of a particular café.
- Choose your time thinking about the chance of getting a seat at busy times and about waiting.
- If there are any rules about sitting for a long time, find them out from the establishment.

### What to do if it is crowded or closed

If a café fills up or is closed you can go to another nearby café or [restaurant](/en/food/bijoy-sarani-restaurants/), or return to the park and sit on the shore of [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/). A bakery or snack shop for something to eat on the go is also an option.

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### What time of day is good to sit

The usual pattern is wanting to sit towards the afternoon after walking; then the light is soft and the setting comfortable. At busy times you may have to wait for a seat, so choose a slightly quieter time. If you return after evening, be more careful.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After the coffee you can take [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) on MRT Line 6 to head to another part of the city. If you want to return to the park, go back the same way; check the timings with DMTCL.

## Café etiquette

- At busy times avoid occupying a single seat for a long time.
- Look at the menu and prices before ordering; do not leave any doubt about the bill.
- Do not behave in a way that disturbs the peace of other guests; if you are with a child, keep an eye on them.

## Who wants to sit in a café, and when

The use of a café differs with the purpose — someone wants a rest, someone a chat or time alone.

### A rest after walking

If you want to sit down and rest your legs after walking in the park, a café is a natural choice. A short rest with water or a light drink and you can move on again.

### A chat or time alone

If you want to spend time chatting with friends or on your own, choose a quieter time; at busy times it is courteous not to hold a single seat for a long time.

### At a glance: what kind of sitting when

| Your purpose | A suitable time | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A short rest after walking | A quiet time in the afternoon | Avoiding the crowd |
| A chat with friends | Before the evening | The peace of other guests |
| Time alone | Midday to afternoon | Not holding a seat too long |
| Sitting with a child | A less crowded time | The child's comfort |

> No specific café is recommended here; the options are only area-based types. Verify the name, menu and price separately.

## Alternatives and combinations

### On a rainy day

On a rainy day sitting inside with a coffee is comforting; but travel takes longer, so keep extra time and an umbrella or raincoat.

### If there is no café

If a café fills up or is closed, use the option of a nearby bakery or snack shop for taking away, or return to the park and sit on the lake shore.

## In short

Coffee and tea are an easy addition to a park visit for a rest — especially in the afternoon. But no establishment's time, menu or prices are confirmed here; verify for yourself before you go, and afterwards arrange your plan to return to the museum, the metro or the park.
