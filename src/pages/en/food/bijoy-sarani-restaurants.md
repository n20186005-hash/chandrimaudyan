After a walk in Chandrima Udyan, if you get hungry there are quite a few food options nearby. In the Bijoy Sarani and Sher-e-Bangla Nagar area adjoining the park you can find various kinds of restaurants for lunch or dinner. Below are some locally known names, the types of food and a general plan for going from the park.

> **Important:** the names below are those of locally known establishments — they are not a recommendation. Their **current opening and closing status, menus, prices or service have not been separately verified here**. Verify for yourself, directly or online, before you go.

- **Thai Chi Restaurant & Cafe:** known for Asian and Thai-style food; suitable for lunch or dinner with family or friends.
- **SOHO CAFE:** a café-style setting; worth considering for light food and a chat.
- **Rüya Coffee Shop:** coffee-focused; according to the brand page it also has a branch on the Military Museum grounds.
- **Cooper's Tejgaon:** known for bakery and food.
- **Tasty Treat – Bijoy Sarani:** a familiar name for fast food and sweets.

## The route from the park

```
Chandrima Udyan (Crescent Lake Road) → Manik Mia Avenue/Bijoy Sarani area → restaurants
```

- **On foot:** leaving the park and following the road alongside, you find food places a short distance away; walking in daylight is safest.
- **Rickshaw/CNG:** an easy way to arrive quickly; agree the fare first.
- **Ride-share:** giving the brand name in navigation helps; at busy times it can take a while.
- **Metro:** getting off at Bijoy Sarani station, you can walk or take a short rickshaw — both are possible.

> No specific walking distance or minutes are assumed here; the time changes with traffic and the state of the route.

### The way from the park to the restaurants

Leaving the park and following the road alongside or Manik Mia Avenue, you reach the restaurants of the Bijoy Sarani area a short distance away. When crossing the main road use a designated crossing, and if walking on foot stay in daylight. Deciding in advance where you are going saves time in a rickshaw/CNG or ride-share.

## Which meal, and what to watch

- **Lunch:** it is convenient to eat lunch after walking in the park; at busy times you may have to wait to get a seat.
- **Dinner:** if you plan to return after evening, you can manage with dinner; be careful when travelling in the dark.
- **Breakfast:** it is not certain that these establishments are suitable for breakfast — light-food places have to be looked at separately.
- **Reservation:** if it is a large group, a holiday or a special occasion, check in advance; on an ordinary day it is not always needed.

## Moving on to the next destination

- **To the museum:** the [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) is nearby; you can go there after lunch.
- **To the metro:** taking [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) to the other end of the city is easy; check the time.
- **Back to the park:** if you want to walk in the park again in the evening light, return the same way; if there are barricades or checkpoints, follow the instructions.

### What to verify before you go

- Look at the current opening and closing status, menu and prices of a particular establishment directly or online.
- On a holiday or special occasion you may have to wait for a seat; for a large group, check in advance.
- Be sure the type of food and the timing match your plan.

### What to do if it is crowded or closed

If a restaurant fills up or is closed, look for another option nearby — a light-food shop, [café](/en/food/coffee-and-tea/) or [bakery](/en/food/quick-snacks/) can work. If you wish, you can push lunch back and arrange it towards the afternoon.

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### At which meals and how crowded

At lunch and dinner times the crowds are greater; going a little earlier or later makes it easier to get a seat. Whether these establishments are suitable for breakfast has to be verified separately.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After eating it is easy to return taking Bijoy Sarani station on MRT Line 6. If you want to walk in the park again, return the same way and be careful when travelling after evening.

## Dining etiquette and the bill

- Before paying, check the order and the prices; if you have any doubt about the bill, make it clear from the start.
- Leave room for other diners; the rules on sitting for a long time differ from establishment to establishment.
- If you want to eat outside the restaurant sitting in the park, put the litter in a designated bin.

## Who, when, what kind of food

The food options in the area are useful in different ways depending on your companions and the time.

### With the family

If you come with family, choosing a place with seating where you can eat slowly is convenient — fix the time keeping the children's patience and the crowds in mind. On a holiday or for a large group it is better to check about seating in advance.

### A quick lunch

If you want to eat quickly in the middle of a park visit, deciding the destination in advance saves time. Going a little before or after the busy lunch period makes it easier to get a seat.

### At a glance: what kind of place in which situation

| Your situation | The kind of place | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet eating with family | A restaurant with seating | Crowds and children's patience |
| Something light and quick | A bakery / snack shop | Takeaway is convenient |
| Just a rest and a chat | A café | Seating at busy times |
| A large group / special occasion | A place checked in advance | Seating and reservation |

> No specific establishment is recommended here; the options are only area-based types. Verify the name, status, menu and price separately.

## General budget and planning strategies

### Controlling costs

Fixing a budget in advance and choosing a place accordingly reduces indecision. Eating something light and then planning a fuller meal afterwards is also possible.

### Backup arrangements

If one place fills up or is closed, use another comparable place nearby or a bakery/café. On a monsoon day travel takes longer, so keep an alternative ready for the way back.

## In short

The Bijoy Sarani area is usable for food after a park visit — lunch or dinner, for family or friends. But no establishment's time, menu or prices are confirmed in this guide; verify for yourself before you go, and after the meal arrange your plan to return to the museum, the metro or the park.
