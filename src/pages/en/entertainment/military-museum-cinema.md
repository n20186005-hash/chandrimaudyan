On a monsoon day Chandrima Udyan has wet paths and the nuisance of sudden rain — then it is natural to look for something indoors. An official source mentions that the nearby Military Museum campus has a cinema, which can be an option after a park visit or on a rainy day. Below is what is known about this and what to keep in mind when planning.

> **Important:** this guide does not state the time, language, ticket price or contact number of any particular show. Such information changes with time — always **verify on the official website or on the spot**.

## What is on the campus

According to the official website the Bangladesh Military Museum campus has—

- the museum's exhibition halls,
- **food facilities**, and
- a **cinema**.

Because these are on the same premises, a museum visit, entertainment and eating can all be done together.

## What is known about the cinema

- **What is showing:** the particular film or type of show may change with the season; check first.
- **Time:** show times change — look at an official source.
- **Tickets:** check the price, seating and whether there is advance booking.
- **Campus rules:** the security and rules of the museum premises may also apply to the cinema.

## Matching it with the museum's timings

According to the official website the museum is normally **closed on Wednesdays** and opens **after midday on Fridays**. The cinema's hours may follow the same rhythm, so—

| Weekly closure | Normally Wednesday |
| Friday | Opens after midday |
| Facilities on campus | Food and a cinema |
| Show time/price | May change — verify from an official source |

> Times may differ because of holidays, special days or maintenance; look at the notice before you go.

### The way from the park to the campus

Leaving the park and passing through the Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Bijoy Sarani area, you reach the museum campus. When crossing the main road use a designated crossing. There may be a security check before entering the campus, so follow the instructions.

## Planning on a rainy day

- **An indoor option:** on a rainy day the [museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) and the cinema together are a good solution.
- **Travel:** there can be traffic and waterlogging in the rain; allow extra time.
- **Preparation:** keep an umbrella/raincoat and extra time in hand.
- **Crowds:** there are more crowds on holidays — choose your time.

## The route from the park

```
Chandrima Udyan → Sher-e-Bangla Nagar → Bijoy Sarani area → Military Museum campus
```

- **Metro:** [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) on MRT Line 6 is the nearby option; check the timings with DMTCL.
- **By road:** rickshaw, CNG or ride-share; agree the fare first.
- **Security:** follow the rules and checkpoints for entering the museum premises.

### What to verify before you go

- Verify the show time, language and ticket information from an official source or on the spot.
- Times may differ on a holiday, special day or during maintenance; look at the notice beforehand.
- Make sure the museum's closure-day rhythm also matches the cinema.

### What to do if you miss a show or it is closed

There is no need to be disappointed if the cinema is closed or you cannot get a seat — you can look at the campus exhibitions, or go to the nearby [Novo Theatre](/en/nearby/novo-theatre/) or the [National Museum of Science and Technology](/en/nearby/science-museum/). On a rainy day any indoor alternative is comfortable.

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### What time of day is good to go

Because it is indoor entertainment you can go at any time of day, though travelling is easier in daylight. There are more crowds on holidays; choose a slightly quieter time. Travel takes longer in the rain, so be prepared and allow extra time.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After the show you can take Bijoy Sarani station on MRT Line 6 to head to another part of the city. If you want to return to the park, go back the same way; check the timings with DMTCL.

## Etiquette for the campus and the cinema

- Keep your mobile silent during the show; if you enter late, follow the instructions and go to your seat in the dark.
- The security and rules of the museum premises may also apply to the cinema, so follow the instructions.
- If there are rules about taking food or drink inside, find them out in advance.
- In a crowd stand in the queue politely; check your ticket and seat number.

## Who it suits and when to watch

This indoor entertainment is not the same for everyone; planning according to companions and time works better.

### With the family

If you come with children, check first whether the type and length of the show is suitable. In a crowd keep the child beside you and follow the instructions to stay quiet during the show.

### Alone or with friends

If you want to spend time alone or with friends, choose a quieter time; seeing a show after the museum makes a full half day.

## Alternatives and combinations

### Together with the museum

Because the exhibitions and the cinema are on the same campus, there is a chance to see the museum and the entertainment together; in between you can use the campus's food facilities.

### If you miss a show

If you miss a show or it is closed, look at the campus exhibitions, or go to the nearby Novo Theatre or the science museum. On a rainy day any indoor alternative is comfortable.

The cinema on the Military Museum campus can be an indoor entertainment option near Chandrima Udyan on a rainy day — along with the museum and food facilities. But never assume the show times, tickets or days; verify on the official website or on the spot, and allow extra time for travel in the rain.
