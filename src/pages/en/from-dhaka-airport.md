Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport is the gateway to Dhaka, and planning the trip from there to Chandrima Udyan is part of your onward journey. Many travellers leave the airport with luggage in hand and feeling tired, so the main question here is how to arrive with the least hassle. Compared with ordinary routes inside the city, here you have to think about luggage, traffic and time together. This is an independent guide; check the current situation before you go.

## Who should travel this route, and when

- Those who want to go straight to the park after landing
- Those with a large bag or backpack in hand
- Those arriving with family or older companions
- Those with only a little time before or after a flight

Travelling alone with a light bag, you can use the metro; otherwise a ride-share or licensed taxi is usually more comfortable.

## What is better if you have luggage

| Situation | Suggestion | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heavy luggage, family | Ride-share or licensed taxi | Official drop-off point, traffic |
| Light luggage, alone | MRT Line 6 once inside the city | Check connections and times |
| To cut cost | MRT + short transport for the last stretch | One change is needed |
| To arrive quickly | Ride-share directly | Time increases at peak hours |

### What to do first after leaving the airport

After coming out of the terminal with your luggage, the first task is to sort out your vehicle. If you use an app-based vehicle, go to the designated pick-up area and wait, because not every car can stop right in front of the terminal. When walking with a trolley, avoid the crowds and stairs, and do not stand at the doorway waving to flag down a car.

## MRT and local connections

The MRT Line 6 stations are separate from the airport terminal; so you cannot get on the line directly from the airport. You first have to reach a station a short distance away to join the line — check with DMTCL, under current conditions, which station and which connection is convenient. To compare the different ways of getting around the city, see the [separate guide](/en/how-to-get-there/).

> Exact walking distances and safe crossings change through the day; check both the airport connections and the city stations before you go.

### Getting to a station from near the airport

There is no metro station directly at the airport terminal, so you first have to reach a nearby station a short distance away — usually the stations on the airport-side end are used for this. With luggage in hand, this short stretch is easier by ride-share or CNG, and allow time at the station for tickets and checking before getting on.

## The stretch after getting off and security

Before getting off at the park and its surroundings, set your navigation to “Chandrima Udyan, Crescent Lake Road” or “Chandrima Udyan”. There may be security checkpoints or barricades around the [parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/) and [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) area, so ask the driver to stop at the official drop-off point as instructed. After getting off, when crossing a major road with luggage, use the footpath and signals.

### Arriving in the evening or at night

If your flight lands late or you reach the park after dark, think ahead about security checkpoints and crowds. At this time ask the driver to stop only at the official drop-off point, and do not walk far with luggage on a dark footpath. If it is very late, it is better to postpone the park visit and come the next morning.

## Planning the return

If you go round the park and then head back towards the airport, traffic is the big uncertainty. So —

- Work out the return early and allow extra time if you are catching a flight.
- Setting out at peak times can cause delay, so leave a little early.
- Be more careful on journeys after dark and arrange your transport in advance.

### How early to leave to catch a flight

Before you go, set the return time with your flight time in mind; on this route in Dhaka's traffic the time is uncertain, so it is safer to set out well before the flight leaves. There may be long queues at the terminal, so allow extra time. Arranging your return vehicle on the app in advance reduces the hassle of waiting.

## Luggage and limited mobility

With heavy luggage or limited mobility, choose a ride-share or licensed taxi for its door-to-door convenience and keep the walking after getting off short. See the [separate guide](/en/park-map-entrances/) on the park's entrances and facilities and check the current situation.

### Trolleys, luggage and cutting the walking

Airport trolleys are useful inside the terminal but cannot be taken outside or into the park — so plan to take a car from the terminal and get off close to the park. Keeping the drop-off point as near the entrance as possible reduces the strain of pulling luggage, and keep a little time in hand for waiting at a checkpoint.

## Luggage, trolleys and pick-up at the airport

The airport terminal has rules for trolleys and checking, so before you leave, look once at where to return the trolley and the designated pick-up area. Knowing in advance where exactly to stand outside the terminal to call a car means you do not have to wander in the crowd.

If the terminal has a counter for organised taxis or a prepaid service, it is usually safer to use it, because the fare is fixed in advance. If you use an app, match the estimated cost and pick-up point shown in the app.

### Two mistakes to avoid

- Getting into a car without agreeing the fare first.
- Leaving your luggage outside the car while waiting for the door to open.

## Children and older passengers arriving from the airport

Rather than going straight to the park after landing, it is more comfortable for children and older passengers to rest a little at the terminal first and then set out, if needed. After a long journey, quickly changing vehicles can be tiring, so going in one car straight to near the park is better.

- For a child, pick up a light snack or water at the terminal.
- With older companions, keep the drop-off point near the entrance.
- Planning to return before evening makes the day easier.

## In short

Arriving from the airport with luggage, a ride-share or licensed taxi is the most comfortable; with a light bag you can use the metro combination, but check the connections and times with DMTCL. Allowing extra time to avoid traffic is the most important thing.
