When going to Chandrima Udyan by car or ride-share, the two most common questions are where to leave the car and exactly where to get off. Because the park sits in Sher-e-Bangla Nagar near the [National Parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/), the [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) and Crescent Lake, the security and traffic arrangements around it change from time to time. So it is better to assume in advance that the place to get off or leave the car may not be exactly where you would like. This is an independent guide; check the current situation before you go.

## Who this guide is for

- Those going by their own car or motorcycle
- Those going by ride-share or taxi
- Those arriving with large luggage or older companions
- Those who use a wheelchair or have limited mobility

## The reality of parking

Do not assume there is guaranteed large parking for visitors inside the park. So —

- Find out the approved places to leave a car in advance and use them.
- Leaving a car by the roadside or in a prohibited place can breach security or traffic instructions.
- Be mentally ready to leave the car some distance away and walk the rest.

> Where parking or drop-off is permitted can change through the day and over time; check the instructions and local sources once you arrive.

### Planning where to leave your own car

Rather than assuming there is a fixed large car park inside the park, be ready to leave your car at an approved place around Sher-e-Bangla Nagar. Before parking, check the stretch by Crescent Lake Road or beside Manik Mia Avenue on your navigation, and do not park where it is prohibited. If you park after dark and walk, keep to lit and busy roads where possible, and do not leave valuables in the car.

### The official drop-off point for ride-shares

Set the app destination as “Chandrima Udyan, Crescent Lake Road” so the driver can head towards the parliament side. Because of the security rules around the parliament building and the Military Museum area, the car may not be able to stop everywhere, so ask the driver to use only the official drop-off point indicated. Do not press to stop suddenly in the middle of a busy road — getting off a little away and walking a short way is usually safer.

## Security checkpoints and traffic instructions

Around the parliament building and the Military Museum area there may sometimes be security checkpoints, barricades or traffic control. In that case —

| Situation | What to do | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checkpoint or barricade | Follow the officer's instructions | Allow extra time |
| Car asked to stop at a fixed point | Follow the driver's guidance | Use the official drop-off point |
| Road diverted | Take an alternative route | Keep navigation updated |
| Crowds or an event | Walk the last stretch | Add time at both ends |

### Before and after getting off at a checkpoint

Before getting off near a checkpoint or barricade, ask the driver to go slowly so there is no sudden stop. If an officer asks the car to stop, keep your handbag and luggage with you and wait calmly; do not stop the car right in the middle of the checkpoint and get out. After passing through, walk in the direction indicated, because routes in this area can be diverted from time to time.

## How to choose an official drop-off point

Under security rules a ride-share or taxi may have to stop at a fixed point. So before getting off, tell the driver that you want to be dropped at an official point, and once there look for the entrance towards the parliament and Crescent Lake. If you have to cross a major road after getting off, use the footpath, a footbridge where available, or a signal, and be more careful after dark.

### Aiming for the entrance towards the parliament and Crescent Lake

Choose a drop-off point that keeps the entrance towards Crescent Lake Road close by; this reduces the need to cross a road. If the car stops on the opposite side of the road, find a footbridge or a signal-controlled crossing — avoid crossing through gaps in the busy traffic. Keeping the walk to the entrance short helps with luggage or older companions, so make the driver understand the direction you want before you get off.

## On the return

If a car comes for the return, ask it to stop at an official point too. At the busy evening hours there is more traffic in this area, so allow extra time for waiting and arrange your ride-share or transport in advance.

### When to call the car for the return

Before returning, set the pick-up point in the app to a fixed, safe spot on the road, because around the parliament-side area the car may not be able to stop on every side. At the busy evening hours the car may be late, so rather than waiting on a dark footpath, stay in a lit and busy place. When the car arrives, ask the driver to use the official drop-off point, and confirm the destination once you get in.

## Luggage and limited mobility

With heavy luggage or limited mobility, keep the drop-off point as close to the entrance as possible and cut the walking. You may have to wait at a checkpoint, so allow extra time for the journey. See the [separate guide](/en/accessibility/) on entrance facilities.

### Keeping the drop-off point and the walking short

Pulling heavy bags while crossing a busy road is hard, so ask the driver to stop as close to the entrance as possible. If you use a wheelchair or have limited mobility, tell the driver before you get off, so that he can head towards the parliament side. If you walk after getting off, choose a wide, shaded footpath and keep a little extra time in hand for waiting at a checkpoint.

## Which roads you take to reach the park or leave your car

Among the familiar roads around Chandrima Udyan in Sher-e-Bangla Nagar, Crescent Lake Road and [Manik Mia Avenue](/en/nearby/manik-mia-avenue/) are among the main ones, and coming from the MRT-side part means heading by way of Bijoy Sarani. Whether by ride-share or your own car, exactly which road permits parking or drop-off changes from day to day; so rather than assuming, going by the destination name (Chandrima Udyan, Crescent Lake Road) helps the driver find the way. If you need to cross a road, use the footpath, a footbridge where available, or a signal, because the crossings in this area can change from time to time.

## A quick check-list before and after getting off

- Before leaving, find out about approved parking or drop-off places from a local source (driver or authority).
- With luggage or older companions, keep the drop-off point near the entrance.
- To cross a road, choose a footbridge or a signal-controlled crossing.
- At a checkpoint, follow the instructions, and update your navigation if the route is diverted.
- Arrange your return transport before dark falls.

## In short

There is no guaranteed parking at the park — leave your car at an approved place or get off near the entrance. Follow the security instructions around the parliament and Military Museum area, use an official drop-off point, and allow extra time for the busy hours.
