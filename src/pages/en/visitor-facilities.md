Whether Chandrima Udyan (চন্দ্রিমা উদ্যান) has toilets, drinking water, seating or shade is a question whose proper answer should come from a verified list. But this is not that list; it is a checklist for you to verify yourself before going. This page is an independent, third-party guide; it is not an official facilities list.

## Why this is a verification checklist

Whether a specific facility exists today or not can change from day to day, and old information can linger online. So it would not be right to state "there is a washroom here" as certain. Instead, keeping your own needs on a list and verifying them one by one is safer, and makes the trip more relaxed.

## What to verify first

| Facility | What to ask/check | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Washroom | Whether it exists, where, whether clean | Not verified |
| Drinking water | Whether there is a filter or tap | Not verified |
| Seating | Benches or a shaded place to sit | Not verified |
| Shade | Shade from large trees or a shelter | Not verified |
| Food | Whether there is a canteen or shop | Not verified |
| Security | Guards, lighting, surveillance | Not verified |

### What information to note while verifying

While verifying, writing down not just "yes/no" but useful details for using the facility helps — such as which way the washroom is, how long it is open, where the water tap is, and when the crowd is smaller. These small notes help you decide later, especially if you have children or older companions.

### How to tell if information is out of date

A photo or description of a facility online may be months or years old. Be cautious with information where no date can be seen, and do not assume it has been "verified"; instead, cross-check it with a recent source or by asking directly.

## What to bring yourself

- Bottled water or your own water.
- Sanitiser and tissues.
- An umbrella or light coat to protect against rain or sun.
- A small mat or cloth that you can spread out to sit on.
- One or two dry snacks, especially if you have children or diabetes.

### Extra preparation for children and older visitors

If you go with children, carry a spare set of clothes to change, tissues and a bottle of water. If you have older companions, consider a light mat or folding chair for rest, avoid long walks and move at a slower pace. All of this is your own preparation — it is not a guarantee that something exists in the park. For preparation with children or older companions, see the [accessibility guide](/en/accessibility/).

## How to ask locally

For reliable information, ask the security staff at the entrance or a local shopkeeper. When asking, be specific — "where is the washroom" or "is drinking water available". For information that changes, it is wise to cross-check two or three sources.

### The right time to ask

Security staff are busy at peak times, so asking in the relatively quiet hours of early morning or midday makes an answer easier to get. If someone is unsure when you ask, do not treat it as "certain" — ask someone else instead.

## Nearby services

There may be some services just outside the park or in the surrounding area — shops, places to eat or transport. But their prices, quality and opening hours all need to be verified separately. No specific business is recommended in this guide. For light food, see [quick meals and snacks](/en/food/quick-snacks/).

## Crowds and cleanliness

Some facilities come under pressure at peak times, such as washrooms or seating. Going in the early morning means you may find these relatively less crowded. If you have doubts about cleanliness, it is better to rely on your own preparation.

### Tips to avoid peak-time crowds

Crowds grow on holidays and towards the afternoon, so going in the morning may mean finding washrooms and seating relatively free. But even this is not certain — the situation that day may differ. If it is crowded, keeping your hands clean and carrying your own water is the reliable option.

## What to do in an emergency

For sudden illness, lack of water or another emergency, it helps to have a few numbers and preparations in mind beforehand. Inform the nearest security staff or the person in charge at the entrance, and find out about the nearest hospital or transport if needed. It is safer to verify in advance which number is whose, because we are not certain about the presence of emergency services in the park. For safety and emergency rules, see [safety and etiquette](/en/safety-and-etiquette/).

## Network and charging preparation

Mobile networks are usually available nearby, but may be weak at peak times or in certain parts inside. So download or save maps and emergency numbers in advance and carry a power bank. Whether charging points exist has not been verified, so your own preparation is the reliable option.

## In short

This is not a confirmed list of facilities but a verification checklist. Toilets, water, seating and shade — verify them all before going, and carry your own needed water and food.
