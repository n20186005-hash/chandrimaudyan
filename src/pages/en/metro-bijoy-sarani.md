Using MRT Line 6 lets you avoid much of Dhaka's long traffic jams and leaves only the short final stretch to manage. For many visitors arriving from the north or central Dhaka, Bijoy Sarani station is an easy gateway. Here is a step-by-step guide to what to do after getting off. This is an independent, unofficial guide — verify the details locally before you set out.

## Who this route suits best

- Those who want to come from inside the city without taking a car
- Those visiting or meeting people in the parliament building and Manik Mia Avenue area
- Small groups travelling together who want to keep costs down
- Those who have no problem walking a short way from the station

In contrast, with heavy luggage or a very small child the final walk can be hard; in that case see the transport options below.

## Which way to head after leaving the station

Bijoy Sarani station lies next to Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and the National Parliament building. After leaving, head towards the main road and aim for the park, looking for Chandrima Udyan along Crescent Lake Road. Because of the security arrangements around [Manik Mia Avenue](/en/nearby/manik-mia-avenue/) and the parliament area, specific roads or crossings can change from time to time.

> Exact walking distances and safe crossings change through the day. Which gate is convenient to leave by, and which route is safe to walk, should be checked locally or with DMTCL before you go.

### The first stretch after leaving the station

After getting off at Bijoy Sarani you go down to the footpath by stairs or lift; which side is open can change from time to time. Once on the footpath, watch the flow of cars and rickshaws and, instead of trying to cross the main road, follow the footpath towards the parliament building. As afternoon turns to evening the footpath gets busier, so keep your bag in front of you.

## On foot, by rickshaw or by CNG

| Option | Best suited to | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On foot | Light bag, used to short walks | Busy crossings, sun and rain |
| Rickshaw | Short distance, low cost | Slow at busy times |
| CNG | When you want to save time | Agree the fare first |
| Ride-share | With luggage or companions | Security rules may fix the stopping point |

If you walk, use the footpath, a footbridge where available, or a signal when crossing a major road, and allow time. If you take a rickshaw or CNG, state the destination clearly as “Chandrima Udyan” or “Crescent Lake” to avoid confusion.

### Crossing roads and changing vehicles

A continuous footpath is not always available from the station forecourt; in places you may have to cross a road. Cross only where there is a footbridge or signal, and avoid stretches where vehicles are turning. If you would rather not walk, you can pick up a rickshaw or CNG right outside the station — one change is enough to reach the near side of the park.

## Crossings, security and surroundings

Around the parliament building and the Military Museum there may sometimes be security checkpoints or barricades. Follow the instructions of the driver or the officer on duty and use the official drop-off point. At busy times the roads here carry a lot of vehicles, so take extra care when choosing your walking route.

## Planning the return

If you return to the station the same way, the evening peak can mean crowds and delays. So —

- Before returning, check the station's service times and last-train schedule with DMTCL.
- Be more careful travelling after dark and arrange your transport in advance.
- It helps to arrive in daylight and plan to head back before dark.

### Last train and crowds on the return

At the evening peak the station gets busier and the exit side may change, so check which side is open before you return. Starting your return in daylight makes connections easier to line up. Check the last-train schedule with DMTCL, then set out.

## Luggage and limited mobility

If you use a wheelchair or find walking difficult, the last stretch from the station to the park may be easier by ride-share. See the [separate guide](/en/accessibility/) on entrance heights, ramps and footpath condition, and check the current situation before you go.

### Getting on and off the train with luggage

With big luggage, look for the lift rather than the stairs and travel slightly before or after the crowd. Do not rush as the doors are about to close; get on and off slowly with your bag. If you have limited mobility, ask station staff about lifts and ramps, because not every side has the same facilities.

## Walking past the parliament building and the Military Museum

The route from Bijoy Sarani station to the park passes the area around the [National Parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/) and the [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/), so you need to watch both directions and security. Walk along the footpath here, do not go over barricades, and observe any no-photography rules.

- Change your route as instructed by guards or traffic police; do not assume in advance which direction is closed at a given time.
- Keep your handbag and luggage with you at checkpoints and do not try to hurry through.
- Vehicles here may be diverted suddenly, so remember your return route in advance.

## Destinations and fares if you take a rickshaw or CNG

The last stretch is easy by rickshaw or CNG, but settle the destination and fare first so you do not waste time haggling at the corner. Rather than standing right by the station, it is safer to step a little away from the crowd to pick your vehicle.

### Taking a rickshaw for a short distance

- State the destination as “Chandrima Udyan, Crescent Lake Road” — or “Chandrima Udyan” if the Bengali name does not work.
- Agree the fare first; no fixed amount is given here because it depends on the time and on bargaining.
- At busy times even this short route can take longer, so allow extra time in your return plan too.

## In short

Leaving Bijoy Sarani station, head towards the parliament area and reach the park on foot or by a short rickshaw or CNG ride. Allow extra time for traffic, security checks and peak hours, and check the station's schedule before you return.
