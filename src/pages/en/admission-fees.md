Whether you need a ticket to get into Chandrima Udyan is hard to answer simply, because we have no verified official ticket information for the park. This page is an independent, third-party guide; it is not official. So it makes no claim that you "must buy a ticket" or that entry is "completely free"; instead it shows how to work out the cost and where to verify it.

## Entry tickets to the park — what is not known

We have found no verifiable official information about the park's entry fee. In that situation, do not take it for granted that entry is ticket-based. Many open parks charge no separate ticket for walking, picnicking or taking photos; but do not decide on that general assumption alone. On the day, check the notice at the entrance and the counter (if there is one).

### Where to find reliable information about tickets

The most reliable source is the notice at the entrance and, if there is a counter, the up-to-date price list there. When searching online, look for government or city corporation announcements, but do not treat an old personal blog or social media post as final. Even when two or three sources agree, check the date, because old information can change. If a post shows a specific price, it is important to check when it was written.

## How to work out the cost

The total cost of a trip is not only the entry fee; travel, food and the tickets of nearby museums all have to be counted together. The table below breaks the cost heads down.

| Cost head | Description | Needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel | Metro, bus, rickshaw, CNG or ride-share fare | Yes — routes and fares change |
| Entry fee | Ticket to enter the park (if any) | Yes — no official information |
| Food and water | Water and snacks you bring, or nearby shops | Yes — prices vary by place |
| Nearby attractions | Separate tickets for museums or exhibitions | Yes — differs by institution |
| Small extras | Rickshaw, toys, photos etc. | Usually cash is needed |

### Common mistakes in costing

Many people budget only for the entry fee, but travel, food and the tickets of nearby institutions together add up to much more. And on the day, sudden rain or traffic can mean extra fares that were not counted. So it helps to check travel fares in advance and to keep cash on you. Small costs add up and have a big effect on the total.

## Separate tickets for nearby attractions

There are several attractions near Chandrima Udyan — such as [the National Museum of Science and Technology](/en/nearby/science-museum/) at Agargaon, the Military Museum at Bijoy Sarani, and the Air Force Museum. Each has separate opening hours and tickets; do not mix up one's price with another's. Check these tickets with the institution concerned before you go.

## Practical budget-planning advice

- If you use the metro for travel, the cost is a little easier to estimate; but verify the fare.
- Carrying your own water and dry snacks reduces the need to buy at higher prices inside or outside.
- Keep cash with you; cards and mobile payments may not be accepted everywhere.
- Set an approximate budget and add 20–30% on top, so that unexpected costs can be absorbed.

### Cash and digital payments

Cash is the safest for small fares, snacks or hand-to-hand purchases; do not assume cards or mobile payments work everywhere. If you use mobile banking, keep network and charges in mind. Breaking a few small notes before the trip makes paying fares or tickets easier.

## Travelling on a low budget

If you want to travel cheaply, three habits reduce the cost: going in the morning and walking around, bringing your own water and food, and seeing only one or two places nearby. Trying to finish all the nearby museums in one day raises both cost and fatigue.

### What can be left out to save money

To cut costs, seeing only one or two nearby places, carrying your own water and snacks, and choosing economical transport such as the metro all help. Before buying an expensive online guide or an advance ticket, think about whether it is really needed. Souvenirs or extra food add to the cost, so decide in advance how much you will spend.

## Avoiding wrong information

Social media often spreads ticket-related posts based on old information or guesswork. Do not believe a price just because you see it; compare at least two or three sources and, where possible, verify locally. Prices change, so it is normal for an old post's price to differ from today's.

## Steps to verify ticket information

First look at the notice at the entrance and the counter's up-to-date information. If you are unsure about the hours, check the [opening hours and schedule](/en/opening-hours/) page as well. Then compare the operating body's official channels, and if needed ask someone local. Note the date of the information, and compare at least two or three sources before deciding. If you find no specific price anywhere, treat the price as uncertain, and do not finalise your budget on it.

## Ways to cut costs when travelling with family

When you go with family, bringing your own water and light snacks reduces buying at higher prices inside and outside. For family-friendly planning, see the [family and children guide](/en/family-guide/). Checking in advance whether several museums or attractions need separate tickets prevents unexpected costs. Using the metro or sharing a ride-share can lower the per-person cost. It is good to fix a budget for children's toys or extra purchases in advance.

## In short

Do not state the final ticket price as certain from an online source — verify at the entrance. In the total cost, include travel, food and the separate tickets of nearby institutions, and leave some room in the budget.
