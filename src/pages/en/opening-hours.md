Chandrima Udyan (চন্দ্রিমা উদ্যান) is an open park in Sher-e-Bangla Nagar, Dhaka, beside the National Parliament building and Crescent Lake. This page is an independent, third-party guide; it is not the official website of any government body that manages the park. So rather than claiming specific hours, it sets out the reliable ways for you to check for yourself whether it is open today.

## How to tell whether it is open today

For any open park, the safest approach is to go to the entrance and read the notice. In Chandrima's case, **no verified schedule from the current operator can be found online**; so if you cannot find specific hours by phone or online, do not assume. Where possible, check with someone local on the morning of your visit, or look at recent visitors' accounts.

- Try to arrive while there is daylight.
- If there is a temporary closure or security duty, the notice posted at the gate is the most reliable information.
- Special days (national events, VIP visits) can change the hours.

### Checking online and by phone before you go

To confirm specific hours, first look at the operating body's official channels — for example the website or announcements of Dhaka North City Corporation. If you find nothing about hours there, treat the information as uncertain, and do not plan to arrive exactly at a specific time. If you can, ask at the gate or check recent visitors' accounts. Note the date on which you saw the information, because an old post's times may have changed.

### What to look for at the gate

On reaching the gate, first read any notice posted or board — this is where the hours, temporary closures and movement instructions are given. If there is a security guard, briefly ask about permission to enter and the hours. If there is a notice, photographing it will help later planning. If no written hours are shown, assume there is no fixed time and that access depends on the situation.

## Why the times on maps are not reliable

Many people plan using the times shown on Google Maps or other apps. But these third-party listings may not be entirely accurate. For example, on 25 October 2026 a third-party map showed unusual times — 14:30–24:00 from Monday to Saturday, and 00:00–12:00 and 14:30–24:00 on Sunday. This kind of listing is not the park's official schedule but information supplied by an outsider; so do not take a specific time for granted and risk it.

> If there is a problem with a third-party map, it does not fix itself automatically. The final word on checking times is always the notice at the entrance and local sources.

## The kinds of hours you may find

The table below is not a specific schedule — it is an outline of what to do based on usual practice and conditions.

| Time / situation | Likely situation | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dawn to morning | Usually the most comfortable part of the day | Good for walking and photos; go early without relying on a set time |
| Midday | Strong sun and more heat | Find shade, carry water |
| Afternoon | Medium heat for walking | Expect traffic on the return |

### How crowds and conditions change through the day

Generally, more people walk in the early morning, but the air is cooler and calmer. At midday the crowds may thin, yet the strong sun and heat mean you look for shade. In the afternoon more families arrive, so the lakeside and the entrance become a little busier. After dusk the crowds fall away and the light fades, so it is worth being cautious for safety. For a comparison of light and crowds by time of day, see [best time to visit](/en/best-time-to-visit/).

## Weekdays and holidays

On holidays, more people come with their families, which can add to the crowd at the entrance and put pressure on parking. So if you go on a holiday, it is better to go early and leave some room in your schedule for getting out. Whether the hours differ on a holiday, however, cannot be stated with certainty without the notice at the entrance.

### Extra preparation on holidays

If you go on a holiday, setting off a little earlier, carrying water and food, and being patient about parking all help. If there is a queue at the entrance the wait can be longer, so keep time in hand for the return journey. When you go with family, fixing a meeting point helps if the crowd separates you.

## Temporary closures and duty

For security reasons some parts may be temporarily closed, and other parts may have rules on movement. Because it is close to the National Parliament building, this kind of restriction is more likely to apply. If that happens, follow the instructions and think about visiting at another time. For the security rules and etiquette, see [safety and etiquette](/en/safety-and-etiquette/).

### What to do if the hours or rules change

If a specific part is closed, follow the authorities' instructions and walk in the open parts instead. If no time limit is given, do not wait in vain — consider visiting a nearby attraction and coming back. If the closure is security-related, the instruction of the officer concerned is usually final, so cooperate rather than argue.

## Why there is uncertainty about the hours

Information on private maps and travel blogs is often old or spreads by copy-paste, so the same wrong time appears in many places. And where information is missing, a site may fill it in itself, which can then not match the real situation. This is why it is important not to rely on a single source, to compare two or three sources, and finally to verify locally.

## If it is closed — alternative plans

If the park is closed or partly closed, the time you have set aside can be used to visit nearby attractions — such as [the museums around it](/en/nearby/science-museum/) or the views near the parliament area. But these places have their own separate opening hours and tickets, so check those in advance too. If the timings do not work out, it is better to move the day back and plan again, because no specific time is certain.

## What to carry

A short preparation list helps before you go — water, a light snack, a cap or umbrella, and any necessary medicine. If the hours are uncertain, keep a backup in mind for phone charge and poor connectivity. Having some cash with you helps for small purchases or transport. From time to time, check the gate notice and the operating body's announcements to keep the timing information up to date.

## In short

The best answer to whether it is open today will be found in the notice at the entrance. Do not treat online times as final; instead, plan to go in daylight, to go early to avoid the crowds, and to allow extra time for the journey back.
