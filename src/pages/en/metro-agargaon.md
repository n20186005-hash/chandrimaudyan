Agargaon station is a station on MRT Line 6 and lies on the near side of Chandrima Udyan. Among the stations used, however, it is a little further than Bijoy Sarani, so the walk is comparatively longer. Many travellers choose this station because a few museum-type destinations are close by, so they can be planned together with the park for a day out. This is an independent guide, not one from any government body.

## Who might choose this route

- Those who want to see the nearby museums as well as the park
- Those coming by metro but wanting to avoid the Bijoy Sarani crowd
- Those with no objection to using short-distance transport
- Families who want to move around slowly

If you only want to reach the park quickly and would rather not walk, [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) is usually more convenient.

## The route from the station and the last stretch

Heading out of the station towards Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and the parliament building brings you close to Chandrima Udyan. Since the distance is a little longer, decide at the start whether you will walk the last stretch or take transport.

| Last stretch by | Best suited to | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walking | Light bag, plenty of time | Sun, rain and busy crossings |
| Rickshaw | Short distance at low cost | Slow at busy times |
| CNG | To save time | Agree the fare first |
| Ride-share | With family or luggage | Security rules may fix the drop-off point |

> Exact walking distances and safe crossings change through the day; check locally where it is safe to get off before you go.

### Which side is better to get off at

Agargaon is a large station, and the two ends lead to different areas — one side towards the museum and convention-centre area, the other towards Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and the parliament side. To reach the park, choose the side towards the parliament and Crescent Lake; to see the museums, take that side. Ask station staff which stairs or lift are open on each side.

### A short ride for the last stretch

The distance from the station to the park is a little longer than from Bijoy Sarani, so the last stretch is quicker by a short ride-share or CNG; tell the driver “Crescent Lake Road” or “Chandrima Udyan, beside the parliament building”. For a rickshaw, agree the fare first. If you plan to walk, find a wide footpath and go slowly, because this stretch carries a lot of traffic at busy times.

## A day out with the museums

Because the science and technology museum and the air-force museum are close to the Agargaon area, they can be fitted into the same trip. A common order is: one museum in the morning, a walk in the park in the afternoon, then back before evening. Museum opening hours, weekly closures and ticket rules change often, so check the latest schedule for the museum concerned before you go. For a detailed plan combining the park and the museums, see the [separate guide](/en/itineraries/park-museum-half-day/).

### Connecting from the museum side to the park

After finishing one museum you do not have to go back to the station side for a ride; you can walk along the footpath towards the parliament side, or take a short ride-share straight to the park's near entrance. Which direction you can leave each museum by, like fares and timings, can change from day to day, so ask there.

## Agargaon's two sides — the museum side and the park side

The identity of the Agargaon area can be divided broadly into two — one part the museums, convention centre and offices, the other the administrative and residential part towards the parliament building and Crescent Lake. Deciding which side you get off on cuts confusion on the last stretch.

- **Museum side:** the [science and technology museum](/en/nearby/science-museum/) and the air-force museum are close to this area; check holidays and timings in advance.
- **Park side:** the side towards the parliament building and Crescent Lake is more convenient for the park; there may be security checkpoints on this side, so follow instructions.

## Travelling with family and children

With family or small children, it is better to skip the long walk and take a ride-share straight from the station to the park's near entrance. Ride-shares are usually easy to find on the Agargaon side, though you may have to wait at busy times. Avoid the same busy times on the way back; leaving before evening makes it easier to line up both the museum side and the park side.

- The walking distance can be long, so carry drinking water and a light snack.
- Look for a shaded footpath and avoid staying in the sun too long.
- Hold a child's hand on busy roads and use the signal when crossing.

## Crossings, security and the return

There may be security checkpoints around the parliament building and the Military Museum; follow the instructions. If you return to the station the same way, the evening peak can cause delays, so —

- Check the station's service times with DMTCL before returning.
- Connections slow down at busy times, so allow extra time.
- Be more careful on journeys after dark.

### Which side to leave from on the return

Choose your exit side depending on whether you are returning after seeing a museum — using the same side means fewer changes. Towards evening the station gets busier, so arrive with a little time in hand. Check the last-train time with DMTCL, then set out.

## Luggage and limited mobility

With large luggage, a ride-share or licensed taxi is more comfortable than walking. If your mobility is limited, plan to take transport straight from the station and see the [separate guide](/en/accessibility/) on entrance facilities.

### At the station with a big bag

If you take the metro with luggage, use the lift rather than the stairs and avoid the busiest times. You may have to open your bag at the station's checking, so be ready. If you have limited mobility or use a wheelchair, ask station staff about lifts and ramps in advance.

## In short

The distance from Agargaon station to the park is a little longer, so the last stretch is usually easier by rickshaw, CNG or ride-share. This station works well for a day out with the nearby science and air-force museums; check timings and tickets in advance.
