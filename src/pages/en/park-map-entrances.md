Before going to the park, the two most useful things are the correct address and a reliable map location. The [name or spelling](/en/zia-udyan-name/) of Chandrima Udyan can differ from app to app, so navigating by the location is easier. This page sets out practical information on the address, the location, the nearby roads and the entrances.

## Address and location

| Point | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Area | Sher-e-Bangla Nagar, Dhaka |
| Adjacent to | The [National Parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/) and [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/) |
| Address on maps | Crecent, Lake Road, Dhaka |
| Approximate location | 23.7666866, 90.3781398 |
| Google Maps | Link given in the sources below |

> There is a risk of error when typing numbers or coordinates by hand; so it is better to use the map link directly.

### A written name versus a direct map link

Using a hand-typed name or coordinates carries a risk of ending up in the wrong place because of a spelling or number error. So opening the Google Maps link given in the sources directly and taking the directions from there is safest. If there is no link, navigate using both the name and the approximate location together.

## Navigating with Google Maps

The easiest way is to open the Google Maps link given in the sources and take the directions from there. If you want to type the name yourself, use চন্দ্রিমা উদ্যান or জিয়া উদ্যান in Bengali and Chandrima Udyan in English. Time-related information on a map comes from a third-party listing, so do not treat it as an official schedule.

### Steps for setting a destination in navigation

1. Open the map link given in the sources.
2. Check whether the destination pin falls on the lake shore or in the area beside the parliament building.
3. For a ride-share, tell the driver “Crescent Lake” or “the park beside the parliament building”.
4. If the pin is doubtful, try both the Bengali and the English name.

## Nearby roads and directions

The park adjoins Lake Road, and around it spreads the administrative area adjoining the parliament building. As a result the area is easy to recognise — keeping in mind which road you entered by makes it easier to find your way back. However, a road may have a one-way rule or a temporary closure, so follow the driver's and local instructions.

### Which road to come by

The park adjoins Lake Road, and administrative roads spread around the parliament building. Remembering which road you came by makes it easier to find your way back. If there is a one-way rule or a temporary closure, follow the driver's and local instructions. At busy times traffic on the roads around the parliament area moves slowly, so allow more time.

## Entrances and security checkpoints

Because of the proximity to the parliament area, there may be security checks around the park. If a particular gate or route is closed on a given day, use the alternative route as the checkpoint directs. If you are asked for a bag search or an identity check, cooperate.

### How to check which gate is open

Which entrance is open depends on security and on changes through the day; it cannot be stated with certainty in advance. On arrival, find out the permitted gate from the signage, a guard or the notice at the grounds. If a gate is closed, use the alternative route as the checkpoint directs. There may be a wait at the gate when it is crowded, so be patient.

## What to check on arrival

- Find out from the signage or a guard which is the permitted entrance.
- Check the notice about permitted parking and any temporary restrictions.
- Read any notice inside the grounds about rules for entry and behaviour.
- Plan the return journey while it is still daylight.

## What to check on your phone before arriving

Opening the map link once on your phone before setting out saves time on the road. If possible, keep a screenshot, so that it helps even if the network is weak. Have two names ready to show the driver — “চন্দ্রিমা উদ্যান” in Bengali and “Chandrima Udyan” in English. If the map shows opening and closing times, take it that this is not an official schedule.

## Where there may be uncertainty

The specific parking arrangements, when which gate is open, or whether there are any rules for entry — none of these is given with certainty in this guide. During a security check or a state event the situation may change. So, before relying on any particular piece of information, verify it locally or with the relevant source.

## In short

The address is Crecent, Lake Road, Dhaka and the approximate location is 23.7666866, 90.3781398; following the Google Maps link for navigation is safest. Entrances and security checks vary, so read the notice on arrival and follow local instructions in the circumstances.
