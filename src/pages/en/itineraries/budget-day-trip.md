Whether you are a student or a traveller on a limited budget, the real pressure of going around Dhaka falls on transport and food. If you can plan the day around Chandrima Udyan, the cost can be kept largely under control — because walking, the trees and time beside the lake cost almost nothing. This route is arranged with exactly that reality in mind. No specific sum of money is given here; rather, the point is how to think about costs and where you can cut them.

## Who it suits, where it starts and where it ends

This route is for students, young travellers and those who want to pass a low-cost day. It starts at the park entrance and ends with a cheap return journey after touring the surroundings. For those who simply want to walk and enjoy the atmosphere, it is the most suitable; setting aside expensive entertainment, walking and the views are the main thing here.

- **Start point:** the entrance of Chandrima Udyan (Sher-e-Bangla Nagar).
- **End point:** a cheap return journey after touring the surroundings.
- **Suits:** students, budget travellers.

### Who this route suits less

If you want comfort, speed or specific entertainment (such as a guided tour or a paid show), a strict budget route does not suit you, because it involves waiting and walking. If you have small children or older companions, it is better to avoid crowded buses and choose the metro. Getting on and off buses or rickshaws with heavy luggage can be a struggle.

## The walking order, step by step

1. Reach the [Bijoy Sarani](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/)/Agargaon area by a cheap mode (metro or bus).
2. Walk the short distance from the station or stop and enter the park.
3. Go around the lakeside walking path; rest in a shaded spot.
4. Take light and economical food from a [nearby eating place](/en/food/quick-snacks/).
5. When your time is up, return by the same cheap mode; try a different time to avoid the crowds.

### Metro and bus — which one, and when

The metro is generally reliable when the traffic is heavy or you want to arrive quickly; the bus is usually cheaper, but the crowds and the timing are uncertain. So you can choose the bus when you have plenty of time, and the metro when time is short or during peak hours. Which routes are running changes often, so check before you go.

## Transport and meals

To cut costs, it is good to choose the metro or bus for travel, because a ride-share usually costs more. Sharing the last stretch's rickshaw or CNG with a companion lowers the cost per person; agree the fare first. For food, choose light and familiar places and carry water and small snacks so that sudden spending is reduced. Do not assume a particular shop or price — check on arrival.

### The advantage of carrying your own water and food

Carrying a bottle of water and light snacks reduces the hassle of haggling over prices or sudden purchases on the way. Carrying home-made dry food (such as biscuits or fruit) is cheap and safe. But do not leave open food in the sun for long — keep it in the shade, and sit down to eat where there is space.

## Opening days, tickets and restrictions

The park's and the surrounding area's opening times, the crowd rules, and whether any ticket is needed all change. No specific fee or time is confirmed here; read the notice at the gate on arrival and check the relevant source if needed. Do not plan around the price of an expensive entertainment or ticket.

> A big way to cut costs is to avoid uncertain purchases and to keep a rough estimate beforehand. Third-party banners, discounts or fare claims are not always reliable — give priority to official sources and directly checked prices.

### Caution over fares and haggling

Before getting into a rickshaw or CNG, clarify the destination and the fare; if there is a fixed rate, check it. Prices on a banner or from a middleman change often, so give priority to officially or directly verified rates. Be careful if asked to pay a large sum in advance, and take a receipt if possible.

## Free and low-cost things to do

Entering Chandrima Udyan to walk, spending time beside [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/) and resting in the tree shade cost almost nothing. Taking photos, sitting near the bridge, or viewing the outside of the [parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/) are also free. Rather than expensive entertainment, these simple activities are the mainstay of a low-cost day. Check their opening rules on the gate notice.

## Saving time also saves money

Avoiding peak hours cuts travel time, and time is a big part of the budget. Choosing the morning or late afternoon instead of the midday rush reduces both the time spent sitting in traffic and the fare. Keeping the return journey on the same cheap mode also avoids unexpected spending.

## If you want to drop something

If the budget is tight, drop expensive entertainment and simply spend the day walking and taking in the views. Bringing some of the food from home saves money. On transport, dropping the ride-share and taking the metro or bus cuts the cost a great deal.

## In short

A low budget does not mean less touring — it means the right plan. With cheap transport, light food, and making walking and the views the main thing, a day trip around Chandrima Udyan becomes affordable. Rather than assuming an exact sum, the wise approach is to estimate first and then spend.
