You have very little time, but you are in Dhaka's Sher-e-Bangla Nagar — so you want to see Chandrima Udyan and the neighbouring [National Parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/) once before you go. This two-hour route is exactly for that visitor, who wants a short, definite plan rather than a long wander. The park lies on the shore of [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/), and the parliament building stands right beside it. You can adjust the steps below slightly to suit the day's crowds and weather.

## Who it suits, where it starts and where it ends

This route is mainly for visitors short on time, couples out for a quick stroll, or people slipping out during an office break. Start at the park's permitted entrance; entrances, notices and opening times change, so read the signs or the instructions at the gate once you arrive. The route ends after viewing the outside of the parliament building, at the permitted exit.

- **Start point:** the park entrance (Sher-e-Bangla Nagar, beside Crescent Lake).
- **End point:** near the exterior of the parliament building.
- **Suits:** time-pressed visitors, people who prefer light walking.

### Who this route suits less

A two-hour route that covers only the park and the exterior of the parliament building leaves no room for museums, markets or deeper study of the architecture. So if you want to tour the Military Museum or a science-themed institution, a separate half-day or full-day plan suits you better. If walking is limited, or you have older companions, keep the two-hour circle even smaller. A continuous two-hour walk can also be tiring with small children.

## The walking order, step by step

1. Enter at the park gate, take the internal walking path first and head towards the lake side.
2. Walk a little beside the lake and take in the surroundings.
3. On your way out, view the outside of the parliament building, its architecture and the surrounding forecourt.
4. Take photos while the light and the crowds allow; move on quickly so that leaving is not a struggle.
5. When your time is up, head back to the station or your car; allow a little extra time on the way back for traffic.

### An alternative order: parliament first

If you want to avoid walking in heat or strong sun, you can reverse the order — first view the outside of the parliament building, then enter the shaded park and rest beside the lake. That way you stay in the tree shade during the hottest part of the day. However, if there are security checkpoints near the building, you may have to change route as directed, so follow the local instructions.

## Time — what to do — notes

| Time | What to do | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 min | Look at the entrance and go in | Check the gate notice and any closing day |
| 10–45 min | Walk beside the lake and take in the scene | Crowds can be heavier on busy days |
| 45–90 min | View the outside of the parliament building | Rules for going inside are separate |
| 90–120 min | Photos, rest and return | Journeys take longer at peak hours |

### How to split a two-hour budget

The real travel time depends on how you arrived — if you came by metro, add the walking or rickshaw leg to your total. For that reason it is safer to keep the internal budget a little smaller: allowing 30–40 minutes in the park and 30–40 minutes for the outside of the parliament building makes the two-hour sum fit. Set aside separate time for entering and exiting, washing up, or taking photos.

## Transport and meals

If you are already inside Dhaka, you can use MRT Line 6's [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) to avoid the traffic, then walk or take a rickshaw or CNG over the short distance. Buses or ride-shares also work, but security rules around the parliament area may require the car to stop at a specific point. Do not assume anything specific about food inside the park; see the [separate guide for nearby eating options](/en/food/bijoy-sarani-restaurants/). It helps to carry light snacks and water.

### A short plan for the return trip and meals

For the return trip, if you go back the same way it is convenient to set out before dusk, because traffic builds as the afternoon wears on. There is no certainty about food inside the park — so plan to eat at a nearby shop or back at home. Carrying light snacks and a bottle of water means you will not go hungry on a two-hour route, and there is less uncertainty about prices too. If you have children or older companions, arrange their favourite food in advance.

## Opening days, tickets and restrictions

The entrance and opening times of the park and the parliament-side area, the crowd rules, and whether any ticket is needed all vary from day to day. No fixed fee or time is stated with certainty here; read the notice at the gate on arrival and check with the relevant office if needed. Generally only the outside of the parliament building can be seen; permission to enter is subject to a separate process.

> Third-party maps on the site do not always show reliable walking times or distances. The actual route may change with roads, barricades and checkpoints.

## If you are short of time or want to drop something

If you have less than an hour, just walk a little beside the lake, glance at the outside of the parliament building and head back. If it is crowded or hot, walk less and sit in a shaded spot. If the rain starts, take a covered path and return quickly, and walk carefully on wet floors.

## Photos, security and crowds

The exterior of the parliament building and the bridge over Crescent Lake are usually the spots where people take most photos. Take photos in daylight and do not disregard instructions from security staff. Some spots around the building may have rules about taking photos or standing; follow the signs or the guards' directions. In a crowd, keep an eye on your belongings and hold children's hands. It is best to keep valuables in a pocket or a closed bag.

## If you have longer than two hours

If you have more time, you can simply expand this two-hour route — wander further along the park's shaded paths, spend time at the Crescent Lake bridge, or add the nearby Military Museum or a science-themed institution. In that case, however, check the museums' and the theatre's closing days and timings first. If you have extra time, spreading the extra part around a light snack break keeps the day comfortable.

## In short

Walking beside the lake after entering the park, then viewing the outside of the parliament building — those two steps complete the two-hour route. Check the opening times and entry rules before you go, and allow extra time for traffic.
