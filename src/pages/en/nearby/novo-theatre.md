After a walk in Chandrima Udyan, many people who want to spend a little more time in the surrounding area think of Novo Theatre. This is a space and science-themed experience in the Bijoy Sarani area, known particularly to children and families. Below is what kind of experience is available here, what to remember when going with the family, and how to fit it in with a park visit.

## What Novo Theatre is

- **Theme:** a space and science-based experience — known as an educational and entertaining presentation.
- **Who it is for:** popular with students, children and families; for anyone interested in science.
- **Location:** the Bijoy Sarani area — close to Chandrima Udyan.

## What to do about shows and entry

> **Verify the time, language, length and ticket price of a particular show from the relevant official source or on the spot.** Do not rely on unofficial numbers or times, because these change.

- **Check first:** before fixing the day you go, find out the current show times and ticket rules.
- **Crowds:** at peak hours or on holidays there may be waiting; going earlier is convenient.
- **Age suitability:** check whether a show is suitable for your child's age.

## Visiting with the family

For children this can be an educational and enjoyable option. But think about a few things first:

- For young children, find out the show length and the crowds in advance.
- Stepping out in the middle of a show, bringing water or food inside — check the rules.
- On a rainy day this kind of indoor experience can be a comfortable option.

### The way from the park to the theatre

After walking around the park and [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/), head out along the road alongside towards the Bijoy Sarani area, and you come close to the theatre. Be careful when crossing the main road and use a designated crossing. If you do not want to miss the show time, verify the current show times before you leave.

## A route combined with the park

```
Park and Crescent Lake → walk/short distance → Bijoy Sarani area → Novo Theatre
```

- **MRT:** [Bijoy Sarani station](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) is the nearby option; check the timings with DMTCL.
- **By road:** you can go by rickshaw, CNG or ride-share.
- **In sequence:** park → theatre → a nearby place to eat — arranged this way it makes a half-day trip.

### What to verify before you go

- Find out the time, language and length of a particular show from the relevant official source or on the spot.
- Check the ticket price, seating and whether there is advance booking, in advance.
- Be sure whether a show is suitable for your child's age.

### What to do if you miss a show or it is closed

If all shows are full or the theatre is closed, do not make a definite claim — arrange an alternative. You can go to the nearby [Military Museum](/en/nearby/military-museum/) or the [National Museum of Science and Technology](/en/nearby/science-museum/), or return to the park for a walk. On a rainy day any indoor destination is a good option.

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### What time of day is good to go

If you go with children, going in daylight is convenient. On holidays and at peak hours the crowds and waiting are greater, so choose a slightly quieter time. Plan to return before dark after the show.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After the theatre you can take Bijoy Sarani station on MRT Line 6 to head to another part of the city. If you want to return to the park, go back the same way. Do not assume a fixed walking distance; check the timings with DMTCL.

## Planning and etiquette for going with children

- It is better to keep talking or sudden movement to a minimum during a show; think of the other audience.
- If there are rules about bringing food or water inside, find them out in advance.
- If you need to take a child out in the middle of a show, follow the instructions.
- In a crowd, keep children always beside you.

## Preparation by children's age and companions

Even for the same show, the preparation differs according to a child's age and companions. Thinking it through before you go makes the experience more comfortable.

### With younger children

Check first whether the length of the show, the dark setting and the sound are suitable for a small child. Find out in advance the rules about stepping out during a show and about bringing water or food inside.

### With older children and teenagers

For older children interested in science, the educational parts can be enjoyable. Going to a nearby museum after the show builds on the learning; but verify the times.

### At a glance: who should keep what in mind

| Companion / age | What to keep in mind | Why |

| A group visit | Contact in advance to secure seats | Group seating may be needed ahead of time |

## Alternatives and combinations

### What to do on a rainy day

Because it is an indoor experience, it is a good option on a rainy day. But in the monsoon travel takes longer and the path can be slippery, so keep an umbrella or raincoat and allow extra time.

### If you miss a show or it is closed

If all shows are full or it is closed, change the plan without making a definite claim — move to a nearby museum or back to the park. If you are with children, it is better to decide on the alternative in advance rather than disappoint them.

> The show time, language, length or ticket price is not assumed here; verify from the relevant official source or on the spot.

## In short

Novo Theatre is an easy addition to a park visit — especially for families and children. However, no definite claim is made here about show times, tickets or suitability; verify everything from the relevant official source before you go, and choose your time to avoid the crowds.
