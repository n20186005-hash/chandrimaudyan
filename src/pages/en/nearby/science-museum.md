Beyond Chandrima Udyan, heading towards Agargaon brings you another educational destination — the National Museum of Science and Technology. It is suitable for students, families or anyone with an interest in science. Below is what kind of exhibitions are here, who benefits most, how to plan on a rainy day and which things need to be verified.

## About the museum

- **Location:** Agargaon, an area close to Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Chandrima Udyan.
- **Content:** exhibitions on science, technology and innovation — these may change or grow from time to time.
- **Target visitors:** students, families and general visitors interested in science.

## What to see and who it is suitable for

| Type of exhibition | Who it is for | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic science and explanatory models | Students and children | The rules on touching/photography |
| Sections on technology and innovation | Teenagers and young people | Exhibitions may change |
| Group educational activities | Schools and colleges | An advance arrangement may be needed |
| A general visit | Families | The schedule at crowded times |

> Verify information about specific exhibitions, times or special activities on the museum's **official website**; no specific time or price is stated here.

## Opening, closing and tickets

The museum's official website carries exhibition times and temporary notices. So—

- Before going, look at the **current times on a government/official source**.
- On holidays, special days or during maintenance the times may differ.
- If there is an entry fee or rules for group visits, verify these directly there.

### The way from the park to the museum

Leaving the park and heading towards the Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Agargaon area brings you to the museum. Getting off at Agargaon station you can walk or take a short rickshaw/CNG ride. When crossing the main road use a designated crossing, and enter through the museum entrance following the notice there.

## Planning for students and families

- **For students:** the exhibitions help to build an interest in science; it is better to go with a notebook or a learning plan.
- **For families:** share out the time taking into account children's patience and the crowds; arrange a rest in the middle.
- **For group visits:** if it is a school or institutional group, contact in advance about permission and timing.

## Planning on a rainy day

In the monsoon (June–September) Dhaka gets sudden heavy rain. On such a day the indoor museum is a comfortable option. However—

- Travel takes longer in the rain; water can collect on the roads.
- Carry an umbrella/raincoat and a change of things.
- Allow extra time for traffic on the way back.

## The route from the park

```
Chandrima Udyan → Sher-e-Bangla Nagar/Agargaon area → National Museum of Science and Technology
```

- **Metro:** [Agargaon station](/en/metro-agargaon/) on MRT Line 6 is convenient; check the timings with DMTCL.
- **By road:** rickshaw, CNG or ride-share; agree the fare first.
- **In sequence:** park → museum → [a nearby place to eat](/en/food/quick-snacks/) — arranged this way it makes a half-day plan.

### What to verify before you go

- Look at the current opening and closing times and temporary notices on the official website.
- Times may differ on a holiday or a maintenance day, so be sure in advance.
- If there is an exhibition schedule and ticket rules, verify them there.

### What to do if it is closed

If the museum is closed you can plan a nearby alternative — the [Air Force Museum](/en/nearby/air-force-museum/) or [Novo Theatre](/en/nearby/novo-theatre/), or return to the park for a walk. On a monsoon day any other indoor destination is also a good solution.

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### What time of day is good to go

Daylight is comfortable for seeing the exhibitions. When school or college groups come at peak hours it is more crowded; choosing a slightly quieter time lets children move about at ease. Allow extra time for traffic on the way back.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After the museum you can take Agargaon station on MRT Line 6 to head to another part of the city. If you want to return to the park, go back the same way. Check the timings with DMTCL.

## Rules and etiquette for entering the museum

- If the rules about no-touch exhibits or photography are written on the walls, follow them.
- For a group visit, contact in advance about permission and timing.
- Walk slowly keeping children beside you; avoid running about in the crowd.
- Ask the museum staff about the rules on food and about rest areas.

## Visit planning and time budget

The museum is large; sharing out the time and the sections you are most interested in beforehand makes for a successful trip.

### For students

Keep time to see the science exhibitions slowly, and you will benefit from taking a notebook and pen or a learning plan. For a group, contact in advance about permission and timing.

### With the family

Keep the children's patience in mind and allow a rest now and then, rather than trying to see everything at once. Ask the museum staff about the rules on food and about rest areas.

### At a glance: what to keep in mind for whom

| Visitor | What to keep in mind | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Students | Share out the time and see it slowly | Learning from the exhibitions accumulates |
| Family | Leave a rest in the middle | Children tire quickly |
| A group visit | Confirm permission and timing in advance | Groups need advance arrangement |
| On a rainy day | Use the indoor setting | Avoids the trouble of walking outside |

## Combining with nearby destinations

### A half-day combination

A walk in the park, the exhibitions in the museum and a nearby place to eat — these three together make a comfortable half day. Adding the nearby Air Force Museum or Novo Theatre adds more educational destinations.

### A rainy-day alternative

On a rainy day this indoor museum is the easy solution; but travel takes longer, so keep an umbrella or raincoat and allow extra time. Allow for traffic on the way back.

> Opening times, ticket prices or details of special exhibitions are not assumed here; verify on the official website and in temporary notices.

## In short

The National Museum of Science and Technology is an educational addition to a park visit — especially for students and families, and a good option on a rainy day. Verify the specific times, tickets and exhibition information only from official sources; choose your time before going and avoid the crowds.
