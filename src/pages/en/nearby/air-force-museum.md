The Bangladesh Air Force Museum, in the Agargaon/Shyamoli area close to Chandrima Udyan, is a different experience for aviation enthusiasts and family visitors. Here you can see various aircraft and aviation-related displays. Below is what to see, how the plan works for children, and what to do about tickets and times.

## About the museum

- **Location:** the Agargaon/Shyamoli area, part of the area close to Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Chandrima Udyan.
- **Content:** various aircraft and aviation-related displays; some sections are outdoors.
- **Official information:** the official website carries the entry fee, times and online ticket information.

## Aircraft displays and the style of visit

- **Aircraft on display:** various aircraft and aviation-related structures — worth seeing and educational.
- **Open section:** some exhibits are outside; in sun, heat or rain you need to be prepared.
- **Educational aspect:** helpful for gaining an understanding of aircraft and the air force.

## Tickets, times and online booking

> **Verify the specific entry fee, opening and closing times and online ticket information on the official website.** No price or time is stated in this guide, because these change.

- Before going, look at the current times on the official site.
- If there is an online ticket or advance booking, doing it in advance is convenient.
- There may be crowds on holidays; choose your time.
- Follow the local instructions on photography and touching things.

### The way from the park to the museum

Leaving the park you need to head past the Sher-e-Bangla Nagar and Agargaon area towards the museum. Getting off at Agargaon station you can walk or take a rickshaw/CNG. Prepare for sun or rain before you reach the open-air section.

## The family and children's experience

Children are generally interested in seeing aircraft, so this is a family-friendly destination. However—

- On a hot day carry water and a hat; in the monsoon an umbrella/raincoat.
- In the open section keep children always beside you.
- Find out in advance about food facilities and rest areas.

## The route from the park

```
Chandrima Udyan → Sher-e-Bangla Nagar/Agargaon → Bangladesh Air Force Museum
```

- **Metro:** [Agargaon station](/en/metro-agargaon/) on MRT Line 6; check the timings with DMTCL.
- **By road:** rickshaw, CNG or ride-share; agree the fare first.
- **In sequence:** park → science museum → air force museum — arranged this way two educational destinations can be fitted into one day.

### What to verify before you go

- Look at the entry fee, opening times and online ticket information on the official website.
- If there is online booking, doing it in advance is convenient.
- Verify in advance the crowds and any difference in the schedule on a holiday or special day.

### What to do if it is closed

If the museum is closed or the tickets have run out you can go to the nearby [National Museum of Science and Technology](/en/nearby/science-museum/) or [Novo Theatre](/en/nearby/novo-theatre/). If you prefer, you can return to the park and spend time on the shore of [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/).

## Day time, crowds and the plan for returning

### What time of day is good to go

Because some displays are in the open air, it is better to go in daylight; choose the morning or afternoon to avoid the harsh midday sun. On holidays the crowds with families grow, so a slightly quieter time is convenient. In the monsoon carry an umbrella or raincoat.

### Afterwards, back to the metro or the park

After the museum you can take Agargaon station on MRT Line 6 to head to another part of the city. If you want to return to the park, go back the same way. Check the timings with DMTCL.

## Safety and etiquette in the open section

- In the open section keep children always beside you; follow the rules about touching the aircraft structures.
- On a hot day carry water and a hat, and in the monsoon an umbrella/raincoat.
- Follow the signboards and the staff's instructions; do not enter prohibited areas.
- Verify on site the current state of the food and rest areas.

## Who it suits

### For aviation enthusiasts

Keep time to see the various aircraft and aviation-related displays slowly. Which aircraft can be seen now may change; read the information boards to learn more.

### With children

Children are usually interested in seeing aircraft, so this is a family-friendly destination. Always keep children beside you in the open section and follow the rules about touching the aircraft structures.

## Preparation and weather-based planning

### Preparation for the open section

Some displays are outdoors, so prepare in advance for sun, heat or rain. Keep water and a hat, or an umbrella or raincoat.

### In heat or monsoon

To avoid the harsh midday sun, choose the morning or afternoon. In the monsoon travel takes longer and the path can be slippery, so allow extra time and keep an alternative plan ready.
