Farmgate is a busy junction at the heart of Dhaka, and the distance from here to Chandrima Udyan is comparatively short. But a short distance does not make the journey easy — because the Farmgate junction and the roads beside it carry heavy traffic for much of the day. So which vehicle you choose for this short stretch decides both your time and your comfort. This page is an independent guide; check the current situation before you go.

## Who should use Farmgate for this route

- Those who want to travel from central Dhaka at low cost
- Travellers used to buses and in small groups
- Those who just want to see the park and head back quickly
- Those coming by bus from the airport or the Gulistan side

With large luggage or a very small child, the last stretch is more comfortable by ride-share.

## Which vehicle to take

| Vehicle | Best suited to | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bus | Low cost, experienced passengers | Slow at busy times, routes change |
| Rickshaw | Short distance, cheap | Slow at the junction, takes longer |
| CNG | To save time | Agree the fare first |
| Ride-share | With luggage or companions | Security rules may fix the drop-off point |
| Metro connection | To avoid traffic | You must first reach the nearest station |

## Common traffic points

On the way from the Farmgate junction towards the parliament area there is heavy traffic, especially during the morning and evening peaks. On rainy days conditions slow further. So —

- Do not rely on a fixed time; allow extra margin.
- At busy times a rickshaw can be slow, so a CNG or ride-share may be quicker.
- Tell the driver the destination clearly as “Chandrima Udyan, Crescent Lake Road”.

> Exact walking distances and safe crossings change through the day; check locally which stop or crossing is convenient.

### Choosing a vehicle at the Farmgate junction

At Farmgate, buses, rickshaws, CNGs and private cars all move together, so crossing the junction takes time. For a short distance a rickshaw is cheap, but it can get stuck at the junction and delay you; if time is short, take a CNG or ride-share. Rather than standing right by the junction, step a little inside to pick a vehicle, which lowers the risk in the flow of traffic.

## The walk after getting off

After getting off the car or bus, heading towards the [parliament building](/en/nearby/national-parliament/) and [Crescent Lake](/en/crescent-lake/) brings you to the park's entrance. There may be security checkpoints in this area, so follow the instructions and get off only at an official point. It helps to arrive before dusk and plan to head back before dark.

### The short stretch from Farmgate towards the parliament building

The stretch from the Farmgate junction towards the parliament building and Crescent Lake is short but busy; if you want to walk, keep to the footpath and avoid the dark or one-sided stretches under the flyover structure. Arriving before evening and planning to return keeps this stretch less crowded. On this short route, with children or older companions the last stretch is more comfortable by CNG or ride-share.

## Planning the return

If you return the same way you may hit the Farmgate traffic again. So before returning —

- Set out a little earlier or later to avoid the peak.
- If you plan to take a bus, check the current stop and timing.
- Be more careful on journeys after dark and arrange your transport in advance.

### Getting ready to catch a bus on the return

If you want to catch a bus back from Farmgate, set out a little before or after the junction's busy hours. Which bus stops where changes from day to day — so check the nearby stop and timing before you head back. If it rains in the afternoon the traffic worsens, so keep a CNG or ride-share within reach as an alternative to the bus.

## Luggage and limited mobility

With a large bag, the crowd on a rickshaw or bus can be hard to manage; a ride-share or licensed taxi is better. If your mobility is limited, plan to cut the walk from the stop to the park as much as possible, and see the [separate guide](/en/accessibility/) on entrance facilities.

### With luggage on a bus or rickshaw

With a large bag, getting on and off a bus in the junction crowd is hard, and a rickshaw has little room for a bag. In that case choose a ride-share or licensed taxi and ask the driver to stop as close to the entrance as possible. If your mobility is limited, it is better to take a ride-share directly to avoid crossing the Farmgate junction.

## One stop by metro from Farmgate (MRT Line 6)

Farmgate itself is a station on MRT Line 6, and [Bijoy Sarani](/en/metro-bijoy-sarani/) is the next station. So instead of getting stuck in road traffic, it can save a lot of time to get on at the station, cross just one stop to Bijoy Sarani, and then take a short rickshaw or CNG ride. If you want to walk, you can also walk from Bijoy Sarani.

### When this helps

- When the Farmgate junction traffic is heavy and you have little time.
- When you want to avoid a long road walk in sun or rain.
- When you want to head towards the park quickly after getting off a bus.

### Check the station connection

Which side of Farmgate station is convenient to get on at, the current service times and the ticketing arrangement can all change from day to day, so check the latest information from DMTCL.

## Things to remember around the Farmgate junction

Farmgate is an old and busy Dhaka junction; a flyover, bus stops and shopping buildings all stand together here.

- The junction footpath has street vendors and crowds, so keep your bag in front of you.
- Do not go under the flyover or into the side lanes; use the main footpath and signal-controlled crossings.
- For the return trip it is handy to keep a little cash with you, but agree the fare first.

## In short

The distance from Farmgate is short, but traffic is the main challenge. To save time take a CNG or ride-share, to cut cost take a rickshaw or bus; but avoiding the peak times and allowing extra time are the biggest advantages of all.
